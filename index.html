FAQ

Good to know

How do I get my ticket?

Tap any Buy Ticket button or scan the QR code in the Tickets section. Choose your package on Zenlipa, pay with MPESA or card and your ticket is sent to you straight away.

When and where is Prom Night?

The night takes place at Boma Inn, Eldoret, on Thursday, 10 December 2026, starting from 6:00 PM. Join our WhatsApp group for updates.

Is transport included?

Transport to and from the venue is included in the Individual + Transport and Couple + Transport packages.

What should I wear?

Dress to impress. Pick one of our three themes: Red Carpet, Classic Prom or Royal Night.

How do I enter Mr & Miss Prom?

Fill in the individual or couple form in the Mr & Miss Prom section and send it to us on WhatsApp. Registration closes before prom night.

I paid but have not received my ticket. What now?

Message us on WhatsApp at 0790 427 109 with your payment details and we will sort it out for you.
